How it works

One price for the group, and a ceiling for every agent.

A seller posts an offer with price tiers. Buyer agents join a pool for that offer, outright or on a condition. At the deadline the contract finds the largest group whose conditions all hold, charges everyone the tier price that group reached, pays the seller, delivers the goods and refunds the rest. If no tier is reached, everyone is refunded in full.

Five steps

  1. 01

    A seller posts tiers

    The seller lists an item with price tiers, for example 1.00 per unit from 1 unit, 0.70 from 20, 0.50 from 50, and deposits all of the inventory into the contract up front. Delivery can never fail for lack of stock.

  2. 02

    Owners fund agents with limits

    Each agent is a sub-account of its owner with a budget and a maximum price per unit, both held by the contract. Its key can call join_pool and leave_pool and nothing else.

  3. 03

    Agents join, outright or on a condition

    “I’ll take 3.” Or “I’ll take 3 if the group reaches 50.” The contract locks units times the price at that threshold. Agents can leave until the lock time; after that the group is frozen.

  4. 04

    The contract resolves the group

    After the deadline anyone can call settle. It starts with every member and removes the highest unmet threshold until all remaining conditions hold. Then it picks the best tier that group reaches.

  5. 05

    Everyone settles at one price

    Each member pays the tier price, receives its units and gets the rest of its lock back. The seller is paid and its unsold units return. If no tier is reached, every agent is refunded in full and the seller keeps its inventory.

Security model

Sub-accounts

Every agent is name.owner.testnet. Deliveries and refunds land on that account and trace back to its owner. Only the parent account can register it or withdraw its budget.

Function-call keys

The agent runtime holds a key limited to join_pool and leave_pool on the Co-op contract. It cannot attach a deposit, move NEAR or call anything else. Owner methods require 1 yoctoNEAR, which this key cannot send.

Contract escrow

Budgets, locks, inventory and payouts live in one contract with no owner and no admin. After the final deploy its full-access keys are removed, so nobody can swap the code.
